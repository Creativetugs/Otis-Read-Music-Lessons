The teacher

A lifetime in music, taught one student at a time.

Otis picked up the guitar at nine and never put it down. He taught himself, toured with his high-school band Big Lost Rainbow, and has lived and played in Rhode Island for forty years.

He produced a series of instrumental albums for North Star Records — Christmas music, sea shanties, hymns — including New England Christmastide, which went on to sell nearly 400,000 copies. Ten albums later, he still starts every lesson the same way: what music do you love?

Students come to the studio at 47 Washington Street to browse the book collection, try unusual instruments, and leave playing something they actually care about. Children as young as four. Adults coming back after decades. Players who already know the chords and want the rest of the language.

Where are lessons held?

In person at Otis Read’s studio, 47 Washington Street, Warren, RI 02885. Online by phone or internet. Travel to your home for an extra $5. Group ukulele at the Tiverton Senior Center.

Do you teach beginners and children?

Yes. Complete beginners, self-taught players, returning adults, and children as young as 4. Lessons follow the student’s goals — a favorite song, theory, songwriting, or simply enjoying the instrument.

What does a lesson cost?

$30 / 30 minutes, $40 / 45 minutes, $50 / 60 minutes. Sliding scale available. Group lessons and travel by request.

Can I buy the CDs online?

Today, orders still go by mail or old PayPal links. This new site is built so the catalog — Christmastide, Wind in the Rigging, Mo’Jo, and the rest — can be heard and purchased in a few clicks.

Do you play weddings, festivals, and benefits?

Yes. Solo, duo, or the Mo’Jo Rhythm Combo — parties, dances, churches, galleries, nursing homes, classrooms, and town squares across New England.
