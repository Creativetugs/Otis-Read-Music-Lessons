Meet Your Teacher

More Than 50 Years With Music

“Experience isn't just about the number of years. It's about knowing how to meet a student where they are.”

Otis Read has been playing music for more than 50 years and teaching for 21 years as a dedicated music teacher in Warren RI.

Over the decades, Otis has worked with complete beginners, self-taught musicians, experienced players, adults and young children. His approach is centered around helping students develop their abilities while keeping music enjoyable and relevant to their own goals.

Whether you want to learn a particular song, understand music theory, improve your technique, start improvising, write songs or simply become more comfortable with your instrument, the lessons can grow with you.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where are lessons held?

In-person lessons are provided from Otis Read's studio in Warren, Rhode Island.

Are online music lessons available?

Yes. Otis works with students remotely by phone or internet for flexible online music instruction.

Do you teach beginners?

Absolutely. Otis works with beginners as well as self-taught and experienced musicians.

Do you teach children?

Yes. Otis works with children, including students as young as 4 years old. Parents are welcome to discuss their child's specific needs before beginning lessons.

What instruments do you teach?

Otis teaches guitar, bass guitar, banjo, ukulele, mandolin, bouzouki and harmonica.

Can I learn specific songs?

Yes. Learning particular songs and repertoire can be part of your lessons, depending on your goals and level.

Do you teach music theory and songwriting?

Yes. Music theory, reading music, and songwriting can all be incorporated into lessons according to the student's needs and interests.
